# How to Import Templates in Elementor

## Importing a Template

1. Go to **Templates > Saved Templates** in your WordPress Dashboard.

![Elementor Saved Templates](/images/elementor-saved-templates.png)

2. Click **Import Templates** at the top of the page, upload the **JSON file** and click **Import Now**.

![Elementor Import Templates](/images/elementor-import-templates.png)

Once imported, the template will appear in your template list and can be used in any layout.

## Inserting a Saved Template

You can insert a saved section or a complete template into any page:

1. Click the **folder icon** in the Elementor widget area.

![Elementor Folder Icon](/images/elementor-folder-icon.png)

2. In the Elementor library, go to the **My Templates** tab. Find your template and click **Insert**.

![Elementor My Templates](/images/elementor-my-templates.png)

3. A prompt will ask whether you want to override the current page's design and settings. Choose **Apply** or **Don't Apply** depending on your needs.
