# Google Fonts Loader

You can load any Google Font and use it for your menu, headings, body text and more.

## How to Load Google Fonts

1. Go to the [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/) website and select the fonts you like.
2. Open the **Families Selected** panel at the bottom right of your screen.
3. Copy the fonts list from the last part of the URL, right after `?family=`.

![Google Fonts URL](/images/google-fonts-url.png)

4. Go to **Appearance > Font Loader** in your WordPress admin and paste your fonts list.

![Font Loader Panel](/images/font-loader-panel.png)
